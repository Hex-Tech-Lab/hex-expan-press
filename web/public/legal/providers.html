This page lists the companies that process personal data for ExpanPress. It is updated whenever a provider is added or removed.

Payment providers (Merchant of Record)

ProviderRoleData processed
Paddle.com Seller of record for book orders: takes payment, calculates and remits tax, handles refunds and buyer billing support Name, email, country, order details and payment data. Paddle's own privacy policy applies to that data.
Other payment providers, as enabled Seller of record for orders on any other payment provider enabled for a book. Each one is named here before it processes orders. The same categories as Paddle.com, under that provider's privacy policy.
