Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

ExpanPress is the sole first-party controller of the personal data it collects directly on this storefront: for example, when you browse books, request support, receive a download link, or opt in to product and release updates. Contact: privacy@expanpress.com.

The Merchant of Record that processed your order is an independent controller of the payment, billing, and tax data it collects. It handles that data under its own privacy policy, named on your receipt.

2. When you buy

The Merchant of Record named on your receipt takes your order and payment and handles your name, email, country, order details, and payment information under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store your full payment card details.

When you buy, we also pass a referral tag to the Merchant of Record recording the link source (the src value) and, when present, a tracking ID (the dub_id value) so the sale can be credited properly. If no source is recorded, the source reads direct.

3. Data we collect

  • Order data: name, email, country, and order details shared with us by the Merchant of Record, plus the referral tag described above.
  • Download records: which product was delivered, when the download link was issued, and download events.
  • Support data: messages you send us.
  • Marketing choice: whether you opted in to product and new-release updates.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser details, and cookies (see section 8).

4. Why we use it

  • To deliver the product you bought and email your download link.
  • To answer support requests and forward refund inquiries to the Merchant of Record.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the storefront secure.
  • To meet tax, accounting, and audit obligations.
  • To send you new releases, product notices, or maintenance updates from ExpanPress—only when you opt in. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data, never share it for third-party external targeted advertising, and never export or transfer raw buyer email lists to third parties or creators. We share data only with: the Merchant of Record for your order (to process payment, tax, and refunds); infrastructure service providers acting strictly under our instructions, listed at Service providers; professional advisers; authorities where required by law; and any successor corporate entity or affiliate operating ExpanPress upon corporate reorganization or formation of our US LLC.

6. International transfers

Some service providers process data outside your jurisdiction. Where required by law, those transfers rely on data processing agreements and appropriate transfer safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

Order, tax, and accounting records are retained for seven years as required for tax and audit compliance. Download records are retained for seven years to support delivery verification and dispute defence. Other records are kept only as long as needed for the purposes in section 4.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We use essential cookies for checkout and security. We set one preference cookie, ep_marketing_optin, recording whether you opted in to updates (kept for one year). When you follow a link to a product, we keep src and dub_id in your browser’s session storage so the purchase can be credited; that storage clears when your browser tab session ends. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy laws, you may ask us to access, correct, delete, or provide a portable copy of your personal data, and you may withdraw update consent at any time. Email privacy@expanpress.com. We respond within 45 days as required by applicable state privacy statutes.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.

11. Changes and business transfers

We will post updates here with a new “Last updated” date. If ExpanPress is transferred to or organized into a successor corporate entity (including a US LLC), relevant customer and order records transfer to that successor under this Privacy Notice.
