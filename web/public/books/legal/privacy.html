Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

ExpanPress is responsible for the personal data it collects directly, for example when you visit our site, contact support, receive a download link, or choose to get updates. Contact: support@expanpress.com.

The Merchant of Record that processed your order is responsible for the order and payment data it collects. It handles that data under its own privacy policy, which you can find on your receipt.

2. When you buy

The Merchant of Record named on your receipt takes your order and payment. It handles your name, email, country, order details and payment details under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store your full card details.

When you buy, we also pass a referral tag to the Merchant of Record. It records where you found the product (the src value) and, when there is one, a tracking ID (the dub_id value), so the sale can be credited to the right channel. If no source is recorded, the source reads direct. A tracking ID, when there is one, follows the source after a colon.

3. Data we collect

  • Order data: name, email, country and order details, as shared with us by the Merchant of Record, and the referral tag described above.
  • Download records: which product was delivered, when the download link was issued, and download events.
  • Support data: messages you send us.
  • Marketing choice: whether you opted in to updates.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser details, and cookies (see section 8).

4. Why we use it

  • To deliver the product you bought and send the download link.
  • To answer support requests and pass refund requests to the Merchant of Record.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To meet tax and accounting duties.
  • To send you updates, only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with the Merchant of Record for your order, to take payment and handle tax and refunds; with service providers that work for us and only on our instructions, listed at Service providers; with professional advisers; and with authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

6. International transfers

Some service providers process data outside the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by their data processing agreements and the safeguards those agreements contain.

7. How long we keep data

Order, tax and accounting records are kept for seven years, as tax and accounting law requires. Download records are kept for seven years. Other records are kept only as long as we need them for the purposes in section 4.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We use essential cookies for checkout and security. We set one preference cookie, ep_marketing_optin, which records whether you opted in to updates. It lasts one year. When you follow a link to a product, we keep the src and dub_id values in your browser's session storage so the purchase can be credited. That storage is cleared when your browser tab session ends. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy laws, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to give you a copy in a portable form, and to withdraw consent to updates. Email support@expanpress.com. We aim to reply within 45 days.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.

11. Changes

We will post any change here with a new "Last updated" date.
