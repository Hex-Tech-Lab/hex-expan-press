1. Who we are

ExpanPress is the controller of the personal data it collects directly: for example, when you visit our site, contact support, receive a download link, or choose to receive updates. Contact: support@expanpress.com.

The Merchant of Record that processed your order is a separate controller of the order and payment data it collects. It processes that data under its own privacy policy, named on your receipt.

2. When you buy

Your order is taken by the Merchant of Record named on your receipt. It processes your name, email, country, order details and payment data under its own privacy policy, [link to each Merchant of Record's privacy policy]. Card details are entered with the Merchant of Record, and we do not receive or store them. [confirm]

When you buy, we also pass a referral tag to the Merchant of Record. It holds the source of the link you followed (the src value) and, when present, a tracking ID (the dub_id value), so the sale can be credited to the channel that sent you. If neither is present, the tag reads direct.

3. Data we collect

  • Order data: name, email, country and order details, as shared with us by the Merchant of Record, and the referral tag described above.
  • Download records: which product was delivered, when the download link was issued, and download events.
  • Support data: messages you send us.
  • Marketing choice: whether you opted in to updates.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser details, and cookies (see section 8).

4. Why we use it

  • To deliver the product you bought and send the download link.
  • To answer support requests and to pass refund requests to the Merchant of Record.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To meet tax and accounting duties.
  • To send you updates, only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with: the Merchant of Record for your order, to take payment and handle tax and refunds; service providers that work on our instructions, listed at Service providers; professional advisers; and authorities where the law requires it. 6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the United States. Those transfers rely on the processors' data processing agreements and the transfer safeguards those agreements contain.

7. How long we keep data

Order, tax and accounting records are kept for seven years, as tax and accounting law requires. Download records are kept for seven years. Other records are kept only as long as we need them for the purposes in section 4.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We use essential cookies for checkout and security. We set one preference cookie, ep_marketing_optin, which records whether you opted in to updates; it lasts one year. Links to a product pass the src and dub_id values; we keep them in your browser's session storage under the keys ep_src and ep_dub_id, so the purchase can be credited. That storage is cleared when your browser tab session ends. You can control cookies in your browser settings.
