Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: refunds are decided by the Merchant of Record that processed your order, as named on your receipt, under its buyer terms. Email us with your order reference and we will review and forward your request within 3 business days.

1. How refunds are decided

The Merchant of Record that processed your order decides refunds for that order under its buyer terms. It is named on your receipt, and those terms set the conditions and timing that apply to your purchase. Mandatory consumer rights where you live are not affected.

2. Product not as described or delivery issues

If a product is materially different from its description, or if you have not received your download link within 1 hour of checkout, contact us with your order reference. We will review the issue and, where a refund is requested, pass your request to the Merchant of Record named on your receipt.

3. Other requests

Other refund requests are decided by the Merchant of Record named on your receipt, except where consumer law gives you rights that cannot be limited.

4. Refund scope

Approved refunds cover the full order amount. One refund is issued per order.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email billing@expanpress.com with your order reference and the email address used at checkout, or reply to your receipt email. We review requests and pass them to the Merchant of Record named on your receipt within 3 business days. Refunds go back to your original payment method; payout timing depends on the Merchant of Record and your bank.
