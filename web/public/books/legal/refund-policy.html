Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: refunds are decided by the Merchant of Record that processed your order, as named on your receipt, under its buyer terms. Email us with your order reference and we will pass your request to that Merchant of Record.

1. How refunds are decided

The Merchant of Record that processed your order decides refunds for that order, under its buyer terms. It is named on your receipt, and those terms set the conditions that apply to your purchase. Your consumer rights where you live are not affected.

2. Product not as described

If a product is materially different from its description, contact us with your order reference. We will look into it and pass your request to the Merchant of Record, which decides refunds for orders it processes.

3. Other requests

Other refund requests are decided by the Merchant of Record, except where consumer law gives you rights that cannot be limited.

4. Refund scope

Refunds are full refunds of the order amount. One refund is issued per order.

A download link issued before a refund stays valid until it expires.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email billing@expanpress.com with your order reference and the email address you used at checkout, or reply to your receipt email. We pass refund requests to the Merchant of Record named on your receipt. Refunds go back to your original payment method. How long a refund takes depends on the Merchant of Record and your bank.
