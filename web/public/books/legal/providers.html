Service providers

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page lists the infrastructure and payment providers involved in delivering buyer orders for ExpanPress. We update it whenever an active buyer-path provider changes.

Merchant of Record

The Merchant of Record that processed your order is named on your receipt. It is the seller of record for that order, collects and remits tax, handles refunds, and processes order data under its own privacy policy.

ProviderRoleData processed
Paddle.com Seller of record for orders routed through Paddle: takes payment, calculates and remits tax, handles refunds and buyer billing support Name, email, country, order details and payment data. That provider’s own privacy policy applies.
Additional Merchants of Record (as enabled at checkout) Seller of record for orders routed through another enabled payment provider, as shown at checkout and named on your receipt Name, email, country, order details and payment data under that provider’s own privacy policy.
