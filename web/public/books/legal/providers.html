This page lists the companies that process buyer personal data for ExpanPress. We update it whenever a provider is added or removed.

Merchant of Record

The Merchant of Record that processed your order is named on your receipt. It is the seller of record for that order. It takes payment, works out and collects tax, handles refunds, and processes your order data under its own privacy policy.

ProviderRoleData processed
Paddle.com Seller of record for your order: takes payment, calculates and remits tax, handles refunds and buyer billing support Name, email, country, order details and payment data. That provider's own privacy policy applies.
