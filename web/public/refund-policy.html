Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: refunds are decided by Paddle.com (or the applicable Merchant of Record named on your receipt), which acts as seller of record for your order, under its buyer terms. Email us and we will review and forward your request within 3 business days.

1. How refunds are decided

Refunds for digital products are decided by Paddle.com or the applicable Merchant of Record named on your receipt, under its buyer terms. Those terms set the refund conditions that apply to your purchase. Mandatory consumer rights where you live are not affected.

2. Product not as described or delivery issues

If a product is materially different from its description or download delivery fails, contact us with your order reference. We will look into it and pass the request to the Merchant of Record that processed your order.

3. Other requests

All other refund requests are decided by the Merchant of Record named on your receipt, except where consumer law gives you rights that cannot be limited.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email billing@expanpress.com with your order reference and checkout email address, or reply to your receipt email. We review requests and pass them to the Merchant of Record that processed your order within 3 business days. Refunds are returned to your original payment method; timing depends on that Merchant of Record and your bank.
