Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: refunds are decided by Paddle.com, the payment provider that is seller of record for your order, under its buyer terms. Email us and we will pass your request to Paddle.

1. How refunds are decided

Refunds for digital books are decided by Paddle.com, which acts as Merchant of Record for your order, under Paddle's buyer terms. Those terms set the refund conditions that apply to your purchase. Your mandatory consumer rights where you live are not affected.

2. Book not as described

If a book is materially different from its description, contact us with your order reference. We will look into it and pass the request to Paddle.com, which decides refunds for orders it processes.

3. Other requests

Refund requests outside Paddle's conditions are decided by Paddle.com, except where consumer law gives you rights that cannot be limited.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email billing@expanpress.com with your order reference and the email address you used at checkout, or reply to your receipt email. We pass refund requests to the payment provider that processed your order, currently Paddle.com. Refunds are paid by that provider, as seller of record, to your original payment method. The time a refund takes depends on Paddle.com and your bank.
