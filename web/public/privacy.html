Legal

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

ExpanPress publishes and delivers digital publications and is the sole first-party controller of personal data it collects directly (for example, creator portal sign-in, signed creator agreement records, support inquiries, download records, and opt-in subscriber lists). Contact: privacy@expanpress.com.

2. When an order is processed

Buyer order payments are processed by an enabled Merchant of Record (such as Paddle.com or another payment provider shown at checkout) acting as independent controller for payment, billing, and tax data under its own privacy policy. ExpanPress never receives or stores full payment card details.

3. Data we collect

  • Order data: name, email, country, and order details shared with us by the Merchant of Record.
  • Account and delivery data: email address, sign-in records, and download records for purchased products.
  • Creator account and identity: name and email address, and Google account identity/name if you sign in with Google.
  • Creator agreement records: typed legal name, signing account, sign-in method, timestamp, document hash, and audit metadata recorded by our e-signature provider.
  • Support and marketing choice data: support messages and opt-in update preferences.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser details, and cookies (see section 8).

4. Why we use it

  • To deliver purchased products and send download links: performance of a contract.
  • To answer support requests and coordinate Merchant of Record refunds: performance of a contract.
  • To run creator sign-in and maintain verifiable signed agreement records: performance of a contract and legitimate interests.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the platform secure: legitimate interests.
  • To meet tax and accounting duties: legal obligation.
  • To send product/release updates only when opted in: consent (withdrawable at any time).

5. Who we share it with

We never sell personal data, never share buyer data for third-party external ad targeting, and never export or transfer raw buyer email lists to creators or third parties. We share data only with: enabled Merchants of Record (payment, tax, refunds); service providers working strictly on our instructions (see Service providers); professional advisers; authorities where required by law; and any successor corporate entity or affiliate operating ExpanPress upon reorganization (including formation of a Wyoming LLC).

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside your country. Where required by law, transfers rely on data processing agreements and appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep data

Order, tax, accounting, and download records are retained for seven years as required for tax and audit compliance. Other records are kept only as long as needed for the purposes in section 4.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We use essential cookies for checkout, sign-in, and security, plus one preference cookie, ep_marketing_optin, recording opt-in status. Product referral links store src and dub_id in browser session storage to credit purchases properly. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under GDPR, UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws, you may access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our use of your data, request portability, and withdraw consent. Email privacy@expanpress.com (responded to within 45 days under US state laws or statutory GDPR timelines). You may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.

11. Changes and business transfers

We will post updates here with a new “Last updated” date. Upon transfer or reorganization of ExpanPress into a successor entity, customer, order, and creator records transfer to that successor under this Privacy Notice.
