Privacy Notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

ExpanPress publishes and sells digital books, and it is the controller of the personal data it collects directly: for example, when you create an account, contact support, or visit our site. Contact: privacy@expanpress.com.

2. When you buy

Payment for a book is taken by a Merchant of Record: a payment provider that is the seller of record for your order. We currently use Paddle.com, and other providers may be enabled for a book. That provider processes your name, email, country, order details and payment data under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store your full card details.

3. Data we collect

  • Order data: name, email, country and order details, as shared with us by the payment provider.
  • Account and access data: your email address, sign-in records, and download records for the books you buy.
  • Creator account and identity: your name and email address, and, if you sign in with Google, the Google account identity and name you use to sign in.
  • Creator agreement records: the name you type to sign, the account that signed, the sign-in method, the time you signed, a fingerprint (hash) of the signed document, and the IP address and browser user-agent recorded with your signature. Our e-signature provider holds the signed agreement and its audit record.
  • Support data: messages you send us.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser details, and cookies (see section 8).

4. Why we use it

  • To deliver the book you bought and send the download link: performance of a contract.
  • To answer support requests and handle refunds: performance of a contract.
  • To run creator sign-in and keep signed creator agreements and their audit records as evidence: performance of a contract and our legitimate interests.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure: our legitimate interests.
  • To meet tax and accounting duties: legal obligation.
  • To send you updates, only if you opt in: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with: our payment providers, acting as Merchant of Record, to take payment and handle tax and refunds; service providers that work on our instructions (hosting, database, email delivery); professional advisers; and authorities where the law requires it. You can see the full list at Service providers.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside your country. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep data

Order, tax and accounting records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Other records are kept only as long as we need them for the purposes in section 4.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for checkout, sign-in and security. We set one preference cookie, ep_marketing_optin, which records whether you opted in to updates. Links to a book pass the referral parameters src and dub_id; we keep them in your browser session storage so a purchase can be credited correctly. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and US state privacy laws, you may access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data, ask for a copy in a portable form, and withdraw consent. Email privacy@expanpress.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.

11. Changes

We will post any change here with a new "Last updated" date.
