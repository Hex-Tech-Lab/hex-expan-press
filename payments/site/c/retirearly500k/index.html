Duane retired on his 59th birthday in late 2021 with just over $500K — living proof of his own thesis: you don't need a million dollars to retire. Before that came 20 years in the music industry (publicity, album sequencing, sales forecasting — work built on hard numbers and forecasting).

Soon afterwards, the market — and his portfolio — tanked. He's shared the real numbers ever since on his YouTube channel: monthly portfolio updates, the withdrawal math, and the honest ups and downs of retirement life, and how to optimize a moderate-income retirement. Along the way, he's built the channel into a collaborative community of people navigating pre- and post-retirement together.

That journey is now available below as a written guide — a practical money guide for the retirement years: the instruments, the order to use them in, and how to recover after a market crash — built on his own real $500K numbers.
